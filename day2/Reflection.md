# Reflection – Day 2

## Date
Day 2 – Salesforce Interview Readiness Bootcamp

Today, I gained practical experience in designing and implementing Apex Triggers based on real business requirements for a Student Placement Management System.

I implemented important business rules such as preventing duplicate applications, validating student eligibility based on CGPA, checking application deadlines, and automatically assigning the default application status. I also learned how to organize Apex code using the Trigger Handler Pattern by separating the Trigger, Handler, and Service classes.

One of the most valuable lessons today was understanding Governor Limits and the importance of writing bulkified code using Lists, Sets, and Maps instead of placing SOQL queries or DML operations inside loops.

This sprint improved my understanding of Salesforce automation, Apex Triggers, and best practices for writing clean, maintainable, and scalable code. I also became more confident in debugging trigger logic and explaining my implementation from an interview perspective.

Overall, Day 2 strengthened my Apex development skills and prepared me to build more advanced Salesforce automation solutions.
