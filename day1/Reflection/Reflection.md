
# Reflection – Day 01

Today I completed the first sprint of the Salesforce Developer Bridge Program by building the foundation of a Student Placement Management System.

I designed a custom Salesforce data model using Student, Company, Job, and Application objects. I created lookup relationships and practiced retrieving data with SOQL queries.

I also implemented an Apex Trigger with a Trigger Handler to prevent duplicate job applications and learned the importance of separating business logic from the trigger.

Finally, I developed a Lightning Web Component that displays student records using the @wire service and deployed it successfully.

Overall, this sprint improved my understanding of Salesforce development and increased my confidence in working with Apex, SOQL, and LWC.
