# Day 5 Reflection

Today I learned that writing Apex is not just about coding but about solving business problems.

I understood the importance of business logic and how enterprise applications validate data before storing it.

Creating separate service classes helped me understand the Single Responsibility Principle and made the project easier to organize.

I also learned how different components work together:
- Service Classes
- Trigger Handlers
- SOQL
- DML
- Business Validations

The biggest challenge was resolving deployment errors caused by API name mismatches and trigger integration. Solving these issues improved my debugging skills and understanding of Salesforce development.

Overall, this sprint helped me understand how professional Salesforce applications are designed using clean architecture and reusable business logic.
